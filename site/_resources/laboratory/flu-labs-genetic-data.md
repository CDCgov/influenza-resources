State Public Health Laboratories use this portal to access the genetic sequencing
results generated from the specimens they send to their National Influenza
Reference Center (NIRC).

Access is granted per laboratory. If you are unsure who on your team already has
access, contact Krista Kniss at [krk9@cdc.gov](mailto:krk9@cdc.gov).
