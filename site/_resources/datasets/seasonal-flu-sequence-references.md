# Genetic References

Genetic sequence references for seasonal influenza (H3N2, H1N1pdm09, B Victoria). Two categories of genetic reference datasets exist, "Complete" and "Contemporary". "Complete" datasets have references across a longer term of evolutionary history of each seasonal influenza type. "Contemporary" datasets are limited to more recent genetic breakpoints for each seasonal influenza type. 

**These datasets are generated on a per-segment level. EPI_SET searches will return all samples from the set, but only the specific segment should be downloaded for sequences.**
