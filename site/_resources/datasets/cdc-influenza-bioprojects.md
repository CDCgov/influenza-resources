CDC's Influenza Division publishes sequences into NCBI's GenBank under a set of BioProject umbrellas. Which BioProject a
sample lands in depends on **who generated the sequence**, **which surveillance
program it came from**, and **the type/subtype of the virus**.

## How the groups differ

|| Program | Who sequenced | What it covers |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
|| [**CDC Genomic Surveillance**](#cdc-genomic-surveillance) |CDC & [US National Influenza Reference Centers](https://www.cdc.gov/flu/php/genomic-sequencing-infrastructure/index.html#:~:text=virus%20genomic%20sequencing.-,National%20Influenza%20Reference%20Centers,-CDC%20funds%20three)| Uniform sampling from diagnostic ["right-sized"](https://aphl.org/docs/default-source/technical/ID-Influenza-Right-Size-Roadmap-Edition2.pdf) sampling through State Public Health Laboratories |

|| [**CDC Genomic Antiviral Surveillance**](#cdc-genomic-antiviral-surveillance) | CDC |  |

Within each program, BioProjects are split **by type/subtype** (H1, H3, B-Victoria,
B-Yamagata, H5, intersubtype, variant strains).

## CDC Genomic Surveillance

CDC & [US National Influenza Reference Centers](https://www.cdc.gov/flu/php/genomic-sequencing-infrastructure/index.html#:~:text=virus%20genomic%20sequencing.-,National%20Influenza%20Reference%20Centers,-CDC%20funds%20three) sequenced samples from uniform sampling from diagnostic ["right-sized"](https://aphl.org/docs/default-source/technical/ID-Influenza-Right-Size-Roadmap-Edition2.pdf) sampling through State Public Health Laboratories.

> ** These samples may come through surveillance programs _**or**_ directly from laboratories that have non-standard diagnostic PCR results.

The **Variant strains** project is for swine-origin influenza A
viruses detected in humans (the `v` designation). 

The **Intersubtype** project is the destination for samples where the subtype is
mixed, unresolved, or spans more than one subtype in a single specimen.
